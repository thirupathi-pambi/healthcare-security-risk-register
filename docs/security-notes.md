# Risk model notes

This is a teaching example, **not** a HIPAA compliance attestation, legal interpretation, vulnerability assessment, or NIST-certified model. Risk scores are subjective likelihood (1–5) times impact (1–5); high >=15, medium 7–14, low <=6. In production, a qualified team must define scoring, safeguards, review dates, evidence, access controls, and escalation rules. Do not publish real vulnerability details or confidential security findings.
